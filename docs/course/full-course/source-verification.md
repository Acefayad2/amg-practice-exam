## Corrections and scope decisions

- Producer CE is due fifteen days before expiration under §10–116. An older MIA chart said thirty days, and a page referenced the public-adjuster provision. The producer lesson uses the producer statute.
- Mandatory prelicensing education ended October 1, 2024; the exam and renewal CE remain. This course is not represented as approved renewal credit.
- Policy-application misrepresentation under §12–207 does not always require intent to deceive. The intentional material license-application ground is a separate rule.
- A covered prosecution’s reporting clock starts at the initial court appearance, not necessarily conviction. The vehicle-law misdemeanor exclusion includes Maryland and other jurisdictions. The written lesson includes both and the charging-document requirement.
- Record retention, hearings, reporting and reinstatement use distinct events and clocks; no universal thirty-day or five-year rule is taught.
- A future October 1, 2026 addition in §2–108 was not presented as a current September requirement.
- COMAR 31.15.02’s defined accident/sickness advertising scope was not reused as a universal life-advertising retention rule. Life advertising and illustrations are separately sourced.
- The $50 promotional-material/merchandise exception also prohibits a purchase condition. It does not generally authorize cash rebates.
- Replacement can involve keeping old coverage and financing a new policy from its values. Variable contracts have a distinct return-value formula.
- Maryland group conversion includes the specified new-group offset, notice-extension conditions and separate whole-group termination rule.

Remaining tax, product and settlement lessons require their own primary-source review before generation. No automated check constitutes coordinator sign-off or a promise of an exam result.
