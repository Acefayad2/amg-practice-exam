- The bars identifying part/time are contact-sheet review annotations below the sampled video frames, not on-screen course branding.
- Diagram and arithmetic checks do not replace the separately assigned legal/factual review. Clear presentation of a legal rule alone does not verify its source.
- No production file was edited because this visual sampling found no actionable defect.
