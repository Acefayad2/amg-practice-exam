## Result

No material factual error or incorrect arithmetic was identified in the 20 lessons reviewed. One actionable assessment-design issue was found: every within-lesson transition between correct-answer positions followed the same four-position cycle. This could let a learner finish required questions by recognizing answer positions rather than applying the concepts. A deterministic, display-only permutation has been implemented in the shared player, preserving stored answer indices and progress.

The result is bounded by the checks below. It does not certify unseen frames, speech delivery, exam results, legal approval or the absence of all possible errors.

## Actionable finding

### P2 — Required answers have a perfectly predictable cycle

Locations: question arrays in all ten reviewed source modules; `public/course/shared/lesson.js`, original line 108, directly renders `question.options` in source order. Example: `lessons-51-52.mjs` has Part 51 correct indices `0,1,2,3,0,1,2,3`; Parts 53–60 use the same starting sequence. Parts 41–50 merely start the same cycle at different positions.

Measured result: **153 of 153** transitions within the 20 lessons satisfy `nextCorrectIndex === (previousCorrectIndex + 1) % 4`. This is an observed implementation pattern, not a statistical inference. Correct-answer balance alone did not remove predictability.

Impact: once a learner notices the cycle or identifies its starting point, they can answer the rest of that lesson by position. This weakens the required question gate as evidence of comprehension. It does not make the written answer keys factually wrong, and it does not establish a fault in the separately shuffled timed assessments.

Implemented correction: `public/course/shared/lesson.js` deterministically permutes each question's displayed choices using a seed derived from lesson ID, question ID and prompt. The original answer indices remain in input values, stored answers, first-attempt records and explanation lookups. The displayed order stays the same on refresh and retry, and explanations use the same visible A/B/C/D order as choices. Source arrays, answer keys, storage keys, state schema and data versions are unchanged, so established records retain their meaning. No voice regeneration is required.

Verification: `node --check public/course/shared/lesson.js` passed. Tests extracted the actual permutation block from the edited player and checked all 173 reviewed questions for complete, unique indices, stable repeat results and preservation of option/explanation mappings. No failures occurred. Displayed correct positions are now A:47, B:44, C:37, D:45; the old cyclic transition occurs 39 of 153 times, rather than 153 of 153. This is an integrity check of presentation, not a claim that answer positions must meet a particular statistical distribution.

Status at report writing: the correction and this 173-question code-level verification are complete. The independent reviewer for lessons 01–20 has been notified and owns the full 60-lesson browser regression, including choosing inputs by original `value` rather than visual position. Refer to the integrated release evidence for the final browser and saved-progress results.

## Consequential factual checks and source evidence

**Maryland accelerated benefits (41).** The definition reduces the later benefit and identifies specific qualifying events, with chronic illness requiring the stated permanent condition. The script deliberately avoids making two ADLs or the federal 24-month terminal-illness definition universal contract triggers. The signed-concurrence, claim-time premium explanation and proportional-value/lien distinctions agree with [COMAR 31.09.16.03](https://regs.maryland.gov/us/md/exec/comar/31.09.16.03), [.05](https://regs.maryland.gov/us/md/exec/comar/31.09.16.05), [.07](https://regs.maryland.gov/us/md/exec/comar/31.09.16.07), [.09](https://regs.maryland.gov/us/md/exec/comar/31.09.16.09) and [.11](https://regs.maryland.gov/us/md/exec/comar/31.09.16.11). The $200,000 benefit less $100,000 accelerated leaves $100,000, even where a discount makes the immediate cash $90,000. [IRS Publication 525](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p525), accelerated-benefit sections, supports the distinct federal exclusions and business-recipient exception.

**Contract-specific riders and income options (42–46).** These lessons consistently label conversion multiples, entry/ending ages, rider amounts and quotes as provided or hypothetical terms. The examples do not import one insurer's design into a Maryland-wide rule. The claims are internally consistent: one child's $10,000 death benefit is not a $30,000 household payment; $200,000 + $50,000 accidental coverage is $250,000; a 3% increase on $100,000 gives $103,000; ten years certain after four completed years leaves six; a $100,000 refundable amount less $30,000 paid leaves $70,000; 75% of $2,000 is $1,500; $60,000 at $1,500/month with zero interest lasts 40 months. These are arithmetic and stated-contract checks, not validation of a current insurer quote or of every cited insurer's full product filing.

**Maryland nonforfeiture (45).** [Insurance §16-504](https://mgaleg.maryland.gov/mgawebsite/Laws/StatuteText?article=gin&enactments=false&section=16-504) confirms the 87.5% net-consideration component and 0.15% floor, subject to the statute's actual formula. The course does not promise that floor as every product's credited rate. Its category limitation and distinction between paid-up value and unrestricted premium refund prevent overgeneralization. The 5% charge example properly gives $95,000 before taxes under its express assumptions.

**Investment risk and indexing (47).** [SEC Investor.gov's indexed-annuity bulletin](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/general-resources/news-alerts/alerts-bulletins/investor-bulletins/updated-investor-bulletin-indexed-annuities) supports participation/cap/spread distinctions and the risk that some registered indexed products incur index losses. The course's formula is explicitly ordered: 8% × 50% = 4%, capped at 3%. It does not equate a zero index credit with no contract charges. The [variable-annuity bulletin](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/general-resources/news-alerts/alerts-bulletins/investor-bulletins/updated-5) supports investment exposure and separate evaluation of guarantees/costs.

**Maryland annuity recommendations (48).** Fresh official text confirms the four obligations, consideration of exchanges in the prior **60 months**, required compensation disclosure on request and the limited role of an unrecommended-transaction acknowledgment. [COMAR 31.09.12.04](https://regs.maryland.gov/us/md/exec/comar/31.09.12.04). New-producer training and equivalency conditions are set out in [.08](https://regs.maryland.gov/us/md/exec/comar/31.09.12.08); this lesson does not claim approved training credit. [.10](https://regs.maryland.gov/us/md/exec/comar/31.09.12.10) specifies **seven years after insurer completion**, agreeing with both narration and L48-10. The donor-annuity permit statement is scoped to covered organizations under [Insurance §16-114](https://mgaleg.maryland.gov/mgawebsite/Laws/StatuteText?article=gin&enactments=false&section=16-114).

**Life insurance taxes (49).** [IRS Publication 525](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p525), life proceeds/installments/surrender sections, supports distinguishing the ordinary death exclusion, taxable interest, premium investment and surrender. The $55,000 less $40,000 example yields $15,000; $200,000 across 100 stated installments gives $2,000 excluded and $400 interest within a $2,400 payment. The [Form 706 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i706), Schedule D, confirm why beneficiary payment does not eliminate an incidents-of-ownership estate analysis. The course states exceptions rather than promising universal exclusion.

**MEC rules (50).** [IRS Bulletin 2007-7](https://www.irs.gov/irb/2007-07_IRB), Notice 2007-15 and Revenue Procedure 2007-19 background, confirms the cumulative seven-pay definition, exchange history, income-first distributions and treatment of loans/pledges. The statute's text in [§7702A](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/7702A) confirms material-change/reduction review and the 60-days-after-contract-year correction treatment; [§72(v)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/72#v) separates the additional tax and its exceptions. All presented MEC calculations are correct: $22,000 exceeds $20,000 by $2,000; $70,000 less $50,000 is $20,000 gain; a $15,000 distribution is within that gain; a $25,000 distribution uses $20,000 gain and $5,000 basis; 10% of $15,000 is $1,500. The post-15-year non-MEC comparison has express limiting assumptions.

**Nonqualified annuity tax (51–52).** [IRS Publication 575](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p575), nonperiodic-payment sections, confirms earnings-first allocation before the starting date, with gain measured without subtracting surrender charges for the partial-payment rule. The course explicitly resets the facts before its alternative $40,000 example, avoiding sequential-withdrawal confusion. [Publication 939](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p939) supports the fixed-income exclusion calculation and post-1986 investment-recovery ceiling. [§72(s) and (u)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/72) support the stated five-year/individual/spouse alternatives, post-start payment pace and non-natural-owner exceptions. The script does not confuse these rules with inherited IRA deadlines.

**2026 IRA figures (53–54).** The [IRS 2026 cost-of-living announcement](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/401k-limit-increases-to-24500-for-2026-ira-limit-increases-to-7500) confirms $7,500 plus $1,100 at 50+, the $81,000–$91,000 and $129,000–$149,000 covered-contributor deduction ranges, the $242,000–$252,000 spouse-covered range, and Roth ranges $153,000–$168,000 and $242,000–$252,000. The lessons label these as 2026 facts rather than permanent limits. [Publication 590-B](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p590b) supports contribution-first Roth ordering, qualified-distribution timing, separate conversion clocks, required beginning dates and post-RBD inherited annual payments. April 1, 2027 and December 31, 2027 correctly apply to the stated owner turning 73 in 2026. The [IRS RMD FAQ](https://www.irs.gov/retirement-plans/retirement-plan-and-ira-required-minimum-distributions-faqs) confirms the employer-plan/IRA still-working distinction and current designated-Roth lifetime treatment.

**Retirement movements and Section 1035 (55–56).** The [IRS rollover guidance](https://www.irs.gov/retirement-plans/plan-participant-employee/rollovers-of-retirement-plan-and-ira-distributions) supports direct-payment treatment, 60 days from receipt, the cross-IRA 12-month restriction and its exceptions, 20% employer-plan versus ordinary 10% IRA withholding, and exclusion of RMDs. A $50,000 gross payment with $10,000 withheld correctly requires replacing that $10,000 to roll the full amount. [§1035](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/1035), [Revenue Ruling 2007-24](https://www.irs.gov/irb/2007-21_IRB#RR-2007-24), and [Revenue Procedure 2011-38](https://www.irs.gov/irb/2011-30_IRB#RP-2011-38) confirm permitted directions, the failure of mere owner-check endorsement, and the partial-exchange 180-day test with specified annuity-payment exceptions. The course correctly says failure of that test needs general-principles analysis, not automatic universal disqualification. $50,000 basis carries in the expressly unadjusted $80,000 exchange example.

**Qualified plans (57–58).** [IRS vesting guidance](https://www.irs.gov/retirement-plans/plan-participant-employee/retirement-topics-vesting) confirms immediate ownership of employee deferrals and SEP/SIMPLE deposits, and the usual 3-year cliff/6-year graded employer schedules. The $5,000 + 60% × $4,000 = $7,400 example is correct. The [early-distribution exception chart](https://www.irs.gov/retirement-plans/plan-participant-employee/retirement-topics-exceptions-to-tax-on-early-distributions) supports the age-55 employer separation exception versus IRA treatment and the SIMPLE initial two-year 25% rate. The [SIMPLE guidance](https://www.irs.gov/retirement-plans/plan-sponsor/simple-ira-plan) supports the disclosed contribution-formula variations. [IRS 403(b) overview](https://www.irs.gov/retirement-plans/403b-plan-fix-it-guide-403b-plan-overview) confirms eligible sponsors and custodial mutual-fund funding as well as annuities. [Notice 2024-2, section K](https://www.irs.gov/irb/2024-02_IRB#NOT-2024-2) confirms current Roth SEP/SIMPLE elections.

**Workshops (59–60).** The independent family need is $35,000 × 15 + $200,000 − $125,000 = $600,000; the separate claim is $100,000 − $12,000 − $600 = $87,400. The standard Maryland individual-life grace wording is expressly scoped and does not claim all policies get exactly 31 days. The retirement workshop distinguishes actual business purpose, direct rollover, IRA deferral and nonqualified distribution tax. A 120-payment minimum after 24 payments leaves 96. At age 64, the stated $20,000 gain-first withdrawal is still ordinary taxable income without the usual early-age additional tax.

## Source-quality and review limitations

- The current IRS SIMPLE landing page still contains an old sentence saying a SIMPLE IRA cannot be Roth, despite other updated portions. That is an external-source inconsistency. The course's contrary statement is supported by Notice 2024-2 and is correct; it should not be reverted to match the stale sentence.
- Legacy unofficial Maryland mirrors returned old 3-year retention and old suitability text. Those were not used to change the course. The current official Maryland regulations confirm the lesson's seven-year rule and revised best-interest duties.
- Direct `uscode.house.gov` requests returned access errors. Statutory provisions were read through Cornell's reproduction of the Code and corroborated by IRS guidance where available. This is disclosed rather than calling that failed retrieval a successful official-host check.
- Contract-specific carrier pages and filings were read in the source modules as citations; this pass did not reopen and re-audit every complete carrier prospectus. The teaching amounts and limits are explicitly stated hypotheticals, not verified market offerings.
- The exact caption-text comparison cannot detect a spoken mispronunciation, an audio repeat or a badly aligned cue when the caption text itself is correct. Root's separate final-media audit is required for those questions.
- This pass does not review the diagnostic or all three timed assessments, other lesson ranges, screenshots of past exams, actual student pass rates or coordinator approval. Those should not be counted in this subreview's scope.
