01 / UNDERSTAND THE REASON

A comparison must tell the truth

Marcus wants to reduce his insurance costs. A producer says a new policy has identical benefits but hides a shorter guarantee and a new surrender charge. Misrepresentation means giving a false or misleading account of the policy. Twisting involves misleading statements or incomplete comparisons intended to induce a policy decision such as surrender, exchange or lapse. Maryland's rule also covers inducing someone to retain a policy through that conduct. An honest, complete comparison can support a legitimate replacement. A replacement is not automatically twisting; deception is the issue.

02 / UNDERSTAND THE REASON

Reputation and financial statements

Elena hears another agency repeat a false claim that an insurer is insolvent. Insurance defamation includes a false or maliciously critical statement about an insurer's financial condition that is calculated to injure the insurance business. False financial statements are also prohibited. Altering an insurer's records to deceive an examiner is not ordinary sales enthusiasm. Compare the facts: describing a real, documented policy limitation is different from inventing financial trouble. Verify information before sharing it, and explain the product without attacking a competitor through false claims.

03 / UNDERSTAND THE REASON

An inducement is not a policy benefit

A producer offers to return part of a life premium personally if Aisha buys today. An off-contract premium rebate used to induce the sale can violate Maryland law. The state permits specified educational materials, promotional materials or merchandise costing no more than fifty dollars, but receipt cannot depend on buying insurance. A twenty-dollar promotional notebook available without a purchase condition is different from cash back only after a sale. Read both parts of the exception. A low dollar amount alone does not authorize a purchase-contingent reward.

04 / UNDERSTAND THE REASON

Fair treatment and risk differences

Unfair discrimination is not the same as every difference in premium. Maryland prohibits unfair differences among life-insurance applicants in the same class with equal expectation of life. Legitimate underwriting differences must still respect specific statutory protections. The life statute has particular rules for blindness, hearing impairment, specified harmless genetic traits and lawful travel. Do not replace these rules with stereotypes or a personal judgment about a community. Collect relevant facts, use lawful underwriting criteria and refer the decision to the insurer's authorized process. Respectful treatment applies to every applicant.

05 / UNDERSTAND THE REASON

Pressure can cross a legal line

Suppose a lender says Elena may obtain a loan only if she buys insurance through the lender's chosen producer. Maryland prohibits tying access to the listed goods, services or credit to buying insurance through a particular producer or insurer. Requiring suitable protection for a legitimate interest is a different question from forcing the seller. Another rule addresses concerted boycott, coercion or intimidation that unreasonably restrains the insurance business. Look for the actual pressure and the required relationship. A client must retain the free choice protected by the statute.

06 / UNDERSTAND THE REASON

Referrals and forms need boundaries

A receptionist without a producer license can connect a customer with a licensed producer. That does not authorize the receptionist to recommend policy terms. Maryland's compensation statute permits certain payments to people who do not sell, solicit or negotiate, subject to its anti-inducement limits. Calling active sales work a referral does not make it lawful. The same care applies to documents. Do not ask a client to sign a blank application so answers can be invented later. Complete the facts accurately, obtain the required review and signatures, and use the insurer's approved correction process.

APPLY / IMPORTANT DISTINCTIONS

Go one step deeper.

Twisting can involve retention as well as replacement

§27–213 covers deceptive or incomplete comparisons intended to induce forfeiture, surrender, retention, exchange, conversion or lapse. Do not classify every replacement as illegal or assume a retention pitch cannot be deceptive.

Example: A producer who lies about a competing policy to prevent a client from leaving can still create a twisting issue.

The $50 exception has both a type and a condition

§27–209(a)(4) names educational materials, promotional materials and merchandise costing no more than $50. Subsection (b) prohibits conditioning receipt on a sale or purchase. The statute separately permits qualifying insurer products and services under stated conditions; these are not unrestricted producer cash rebates.

Example: A qualifying notebook offered regardless of purchase and a premium refund contingent on purchase require different analysis.

Specific life underwriting protections

Under §27–208(a), actuarial justification may be considered for a physical handicap or disability other than blindness or hearing impairment. The statute also addresses specified harmless genetic traits. Past lawful travel cannot alone support the listed adverse life-insurance actions. Specific future lawful travel can be treated differently only under the stated substantiated-risk conditions; a State Department advisory cannot be the sole supporting data source.

Example: Do not use a blanket rule that all travel or all disability automatically justifies a higher life rate.

Ordinary recordkeeping is not financial falsification

§27–205 addresses knowingly false statements of insurer financial condition and deceptive false entries or material omissions in insurer records. §27–204 addresses the specified injurious statements about financial condition.

Example: An honest correction of an accounting error differs from hiding a liability to fool an examiner.

A referral label cannot authorize selling

Under §10–130, review what a recipient actually does and the applicable payment exception. This lesson does not create a blanket dollar limit for all referrals or permission to compensate unlicensed negotiation. Blank signed forms create a separate accuracy and signature-control problem; follow truthful application and insurer correction requirements.

Example: The receptionist schedules Marcus’s meeting; the licensed producer explains coverage and makes the recommendation.
