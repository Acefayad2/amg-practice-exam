01 / UNDERSTAND THE REASON

Three labels can describe one insurer

Elena reviews a life insurer formed in Pennsylvania and authorized to operate in Maryland. Its shareholders own the company. Is it stock, foreign, or admitted? It can be all three. Stock describes ownership. Foreign describes where the company was formed relative to Maryland. Admitted describes authorization in Maryland. Exam questions often put these separate dimensions together. Begin by asking which dimension the question actually tests. A company's location, ownership and permission to transact business are not interchangeable labels.

02 / UNDERSTAND THE REASON

Who owns the organization?

A stock insurer is owned by shareholders. A mutual insurer is owned by its policyholders, with rights determined by its structure and law. Assessment arrangements may permit additional assessments of members under their terms; do not assume that every mutual policy allows an extra assessment. Fraternal benefit societies are membership organizations organized around a common bond and operate under special laws. They can provide insurance benefits to members. Private insurers differ from government programs, whose eligibility and benefits come from their governing laws and program rules.

03 / UNDERSTAND THE REASON

Where was it formed?

Domicile refers to the jurisdiction of formation. For a Maryland question, an insurer formed in Maryland is domestic. One formed in another U.S. state is foreign. One formed outside the United States is alien. These terms say nothing by themselves about whether the company is licensed in Maryland. A foreign or alien insurer can be admitted. An office in Baltimore does not turn a Pennsylvania corporation into a Maryland domestic insurer. The formation jurisdiction, rather than the producer's office, controls this classification.

04 / UNDERSTAND THE REASON

Permission and financial strength

An admitted insurer holds authority to transact the authorized insurance business in the state. Nonadmitted means it lacks that authority. This is not a shortcut for placing ordinary life insurance with any unlicensed company. Verify the insurer and the producer's authority for the transaction. Financial-strength ratings are opinions from rating organizations, not government promises that every benefit will be paid. Ratings, capital, earnings and operating results can change. Guaranty protection is a separate statutory system with eligibility rules, exclusions and limits.

05 / UNDERSTAND THE REASON

How the policy reaches the customer

Insurers distribute coverage through several systems. Captive or exclusive producers generally represent one insurer or an affiliated group. Independent producers can represent multiple insurers, subject to their contracts and appointments. Direct-response distribution can use the internet, mail or telephone without the same producer relationship. The label independent does not mean authority to bind every insurer. A purchasing group is a group formed to buy insurance; it is not automatically an insurer. Federal risk purchasing groups chiefly concern liability insurance, so do not use that label as an ordinary life-placement exception.

06 / UNDERSTAND THE REASON

Classify before comparing

Return to Elena's Pennsylvania company. It is foreign relative to Maryland, stock because shareholders own it, and admitted because Maryland has authorized it. If a rating organization gives it a strong rating, that adds an opinion about financial strength, not another type of license. If a producer represents the company, check that producer's authority separately. Keep five questions distinct: who owns the insurer, where was it formed, where may it operate, how does it distribute coverage, and what do current financial measures show?

APPLY / IMPORTANT DISTINCTIONS

Go one step deeper.

Acting for an unauthorized insurer is a separate violation

Maryland Insurance §4-203 generally prohibits directly or indirectly acting for or assisting an unauthorized insurer with Maryland risks, including soliciting or negotiating coverage, collecting premiums and otherwise transacting insurance. The statute identifies specific exceptions. A producer license, insurer rating or customer consent does not by itself create one.

Example: For an ordinary Maryland life placement with no applicable exception, decline the transaction and verify insurer and producer authority before proceeding.

Government programs remain separate

Programs such as federal employees’ group life and veterans’ life coverage have specific eligibility, enrollment and continuation rules. They are not substitutes for verifying a private insurer’s certificate of authority. Government life programs are taught further in Part 31.

Example: A federal employee’s program eligibility comes from employment and program rules, not from a private carrier’s marketing label.

Purchasing groups do not become benefit-paying insurers

A group can obtain coverage from an insurer without itself taking on the insurer’s underwriting obligation. Do not confuse a purchasing group, a master-policyholder and an insurer. The federal liability-risk-purchasing framework is not general authority to transact unauthorized life business.

Example: An association arranging coverage for members may hold a master policy; the licensed insurer remains the carrier named in the contract.
