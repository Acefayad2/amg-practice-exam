01 / UNDERSTAND THE REASON

Replacement is broader than cancellation

Marcus is offered a new life policy. He plans to reduce his existing policy to paid-up coverage so he can afford the new premium. That can be a replacement even though the old policy is not completely canceled. Maryland's definition includes specified reductions, surrenders, lapses and other changes caused by buying new coverage. The test concerns what the proposing producer knows or should know about the transaction. Start by asking what existing coverage the applicant has and exactly how the new purchase will affect it.

02 / UNDERSTAND THE REASON

Borrowing can finance a replacement

Aisha wants to keep her old policy, borrow from its cash value and use that money to pay the new life policy's premium. This is a financed purchase under the replacement definition. Keeping both policies does not remove the issue. The borrowing can reduce available values and affect the amount eventually payable under the old policy. Compare the continuing costs of both contracts, the loan interest and the consequences if the plan performs differently than expected. A producer should not describe borrowed policy money as a free new benefit.

03 / UNDERSTAND THE REASON

The producer documents the facts

For a covered application, the applicant and producer sign a statement about existing life insurance or annuities. If existing coverage is present, the producer presents the replacement notice no later than taking the application and reads it unless the applicant declines that reading. The notice is signed and left with the applicant. It identifies the affected contracts and whether each is replaced or used for financing. Required sales materials also go to the applicant and the insurer. Do not wait until delivery to discover an undisclosed replacement.

04 / UNDERSTAND THE REASON

The new insurer has its own duties

The replacing insurer verifies the required forms and notifies another affected existing insurer within five business days after the specified completed-application receipt or identification of the replacement. When the existing insurer requests the proposed illustration or summary, another five-business-day requirement applies to providing the available document. Registered variable contracts have a specific alternative involving contributions and prospectus identification. The producer's paperwork does not replace these insurer duties. Keeping responsibilities separate helps you answer a question that names the insurer, the producer or the applicant.

05 / UNDERSTAND THE REASON

The replacement return right

For a covered replacement, the owner receives notice of a right to return the new contract within thirty days after delivery. The rule ordinarily provides a full refund of premiums or considerations, including fees and charges. Variable contracts use a different formula: cash surrender value plus the specified deducted or imposed fees and charges. Do not promise that variable investment losses disappear. The chapter also gives credit for elapsed contestability and suicide periods in specified same-insurer or affiliated-insurer replacements, within the stated amount limits. Check the actual transaction.

06 / UNDERSTAND THE REASON

Compare the complete outcome

Marcus should compare both policies before giving up his old coverage. He needs to understand whether the new coverage is effective, what guarantees change, which costs restart and how existing loans affect the result. Changed health can affect new underwriting. A replacement may meet a real need, but a sales illustration alone is not a complete comparison. Maryland also exempts specified transactions from this chapter, such as certain contractual conversions. Identify the scope before applying a deadline. The professional goal is an informed decision supported by accurate documents, not replacement for its own sake.

APPLY / IMPORTANT DISTINCTIONS

Go one step deeper.

The old contract need not disappear

Replacement can include reduced paid-up coverage, extended term, reduced benefits or duration, reduced cash-value reissue and financed purchases when caused by the new transaction and known or reasonably knowable to the proposing party. Existing coverage can include a policy under a qualifying receipt or refund period.

Example: An owner uses an old policy’s values to fund a new policy and retains the old policy. The financing can still make it a replacement.

Review the chapter’s exclusions

COMAR 31.09.05.02 lists exclusions with conditions: credit life, specified group transactions without direct solicitation, qualifying contractual changes or conversions, specified employer-funded arrangements and other listed transactions. A nonconvertible term policy expiring within five years or less and not renewable is another specified exclusion. Do not turn one narrow exclusion into a rule for every transaction of that general type.

Example: A qualifying contractual term conversion is different from buying unrelated coverage and surrendering the old contract.

Sales-material delivery has a special electronic rule

For a replacement, the producer leaves the original or a copy of the required sales materials at completion of the application. Electronically presented materials must be provided in printed form no later than delivery. Required documents, identified approved material and individualized materials are submitted under the rule, subject to its stated alternative.

Example: An electronic presentation does not excuse leaving the applicant without the required record of what was shown.

Retention depends on the document and party

The insurer’s production-of-information rule covers the listed sales materials, illustrations and signed statements for at least five years after termination or expiration of the proposed contract. Replacing and existing insurers have notification retention duties lasting at least five years or through the applicable next market-conduct examination, whichever is later.

Example: Five years from the application date is not a universal replacement-record retention period.

Existing-policy values need their own warning

Under COMAR 31.09.05.07(C), a request to borrow, surrender or withdraw life values triggers the existing insurer’s notice within fourteen business days explaining possible effects on policy elements and values. A separate notice is required when the check goes to someone other than the owner. Consecutive automatic premium loans have a first-loan notice rule.

Example: Moving value out of a policy can alter more than the immediately available cash.

A replacement does not have one automatic tax result

A surrender, loan, distribution or qualifying exchange can have different consequences. The tax and §1035 lessons explain those distinctions. A proper replacement notice is not a ruling that the transaction is tax-free.

Example: A producer does not promise tax-free treatment merely because the old and new contracts are both life insurance.
