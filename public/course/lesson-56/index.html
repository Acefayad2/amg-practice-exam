01 / UNDERSTAND THE REASON

An exchange is a defined tax transaction

Rosa considers moving value from a personally owned nonqualified annuity into a different annuity. Section ten thirty-five can allow a qualifying exchange without recognizing the existing gain at the time of the exchange. It does not mean every transfer is eligible, every future payment is excluded or every cost disappears. The original contract category, the new contract category, the insured or ownership interests and the actual transaction method matter. A tax-favored exchange also is different from a retirement-account rollover. Start with which contracts are involved and how the insurers will carry out the exchange before promising a tax result.

02 / UNDERSTAND THE REASON

The direction of the exchange matters

Within the core life and annuity categories, a life insurance contract can be exchanged for another life contract, an endowment or an annuity when the other requirements are met. An endowment can go to an eligible endowment or annuity. An annuity can go to another annuity. The reverse move from an annuity to life insurance does not qualify under this section. Neither does an annuity-to-endowment exchange. An endowment-to-endowment exchange has an additional requirement: the new regular payments must begin no later than they would under the old contract. The statute includes specified qualified long-term-care directions as well; that is a separate contract category, not permission to exchange any insurance product for any other.

03 / UNDERSTAND THE REASON

Keep the required insured and ownership identity

Marcus asks whether he can use an exchange to replace insurance on his own life with insurance on a different person. The exchange rules require the relevant insured identity to remain consistent; a different insured is not simply the same investment in a new wrapper. For annuity exchanges, the regulation also requires the same person or persons to remain entitled under the contracts as the obligees. Do not casually add or remove an owner while assuming tax treatment remains unchanged. The insurer must review the actual registration and contract facts. A new product name, an attractive illustration or the same premium amount does not substitute for meeting the identity requirements.

04 / UNDERSTAND THE REASON

Cash received and reinvested is not the same exchange

Rosa asks the old insurer to send her the surrender money so she can buy the new annuity herself. That is not the same as an actual direct exchange or assignment processed between the insurers. An IRS ruling addressed a check issued to the owner and then endorsed to the second insurer: that endorsement did not turn the payment into a qualifying exchange. The nonqualified annuity payment remained taxable to the extent required by its distribution rules. Do not promise the sixty-day retirement rollover rule for this personal contract. Arrange the proper exchange process before the owner takes a distribution, and confirm that both insurers will handle the transaction as intended.

05 / UNDERSTAND THE REASON

Deferred gain and basis follow the qualifying transaction

Assume Rosa exchanges an annuity worth eighty thousand dollars with fifty thousand dollars of adjusted investment for a new annuity of equal value. The question states that all exchange requirements are met, with no cash received, debt, charges or other adjustment. The thirty thousand dollars of gain is not recognized currently, and the fifty thousand dollar basis carries into the replacement under these facts. It does not become eighty thousand dollars of fresh after-tax investment. Receiving cash or discharging policy debt can introduce taxable gain under additional rules. A life policy received in exchange for a modified endowment contract also retains that classification under the applicable rule. Check these features before using the simple example.

06 / UNDERSTAND THE REASON

Tax eligibility and replacement quality are separate decisions

A qualifying exchange can still involve surrender charges, new restrictions, lost benefits, different guarantees or higher ongoing expenses. Rosa should compare the entire transaction with keeping the existing contract. For a covered Maryland annuity recommendation, the best-interest and replacement review includes the actual costs, benefits and required consumer information. A producer should not describe tax nonrecognition as proof that the replacement is better. Partial annuity exchanges have additional rules, including a timing test for later distributions, so they should not be treated as automatically identical to a complete exchange. On an exam, identify the allowed direction, correct method and carryover result, then keep the separate contract and recommendation consequences in view.

APPLY / IMPORTANT DISTINCTIONS

Go one step deeper.

The current statute includes specified LTC directions

Section 1035(a) permits qualifying life, endowment and annuity exchanges into a qualified long-term-care contract, and qualified LTC into qualified LTC. This does not permit any general health-policy exchange. The present course continues to focus on life, annuities and the associated exam outline.

Example: The older regulation’s abbreviated category list is read together with the current statute.

Annuity-to-life is an important prohibited reverse direction

The life-to-annuity direction can qualify; annuity-to-life cannot qualify under §1035. Endowment-to-endowment also requires the new payment date not to be later. Identity and other transaction requirements still apply to a permitted category pair.

Example: Tax eligibility is not established by matching the premium amounts.

The check ruling concerns actual payment to the owner

Revenue Ruling 2007-24 rejects nonrecognition when an insurer pays a nonqualified annuity check to the owner and the owner merely endorses it to the new insurer. It distinguishes proper direct exchanges and assignments. Retirement-account rollover provisions do not automatically cover personal nonqualified annuity cash.

Example: Arrange the exchange before requesting a surrender payment to the owner.

Partial annuity exchanges have a particular timing test

Revenue Procedure 2011-38 provides a 180-day test for covered direct partial exchanges. It allows specified annuity payments for ten years or more or one or more lives; otherwise payments from either contract in that period require further review. Failing that test invokes general tax principles and the facts, rather than an automatic universal characterization.

Example: Do not teach an unconditional one-year holding requirement or call every partial withdrawal automatically disqualifying.

Carryover is not a basis reset

The $80,000/$50,000 example assumes no cash, debt, charges or other adjustment. Money or other property received, including certain debt discharge, can require gain recognition and different basis calculations. An exchange from a MEC does not cleanse MEC history.

Example: A new contract can have different costs and benefits even when current gain is not recognized.
